# CMS Platform Comparison for Community Football Clubs

Use this when a club's current website platform is holding them back, or when building from scratch. The right platform depends on who will maintain the site and what the club needs.

---

## The Core Question: Who will update this site?

| Maintainer profile | Recommended platform |
|---|---|
| Volunteer with no tech skills | Squarespace or Wix |
| Committee member comfortable with basic tools | Squarespace or WordPress.com |
| Someone with a bit of tech confidence | Webflow |
| Developer volunteer | WordPress self-hosted or Webflow |

---

## Platform Comparison

### Squarespace ⭐ Recommended for most clubs
**Cost:** ~$23–$33 AUD/month (approx. $276–$396/year)
**Best for:** Clubs that want a beautiful, professional site without technical headaches.

**Pros:**
- Templates look genuinely professional out of the box
- Drag-and-drop editing — anyone can update content
- SSL included automatically
- Mobile-optimised automatically
- Built-in analytics
- Good image handling
- Reliable hosting

**Cons:**
- Less flexible than custom builds
- Monthly cost (compare to a one-time $500 custom site)
- No free tier (though 14-day trial available)

**Verdict:** The best balance of quality, ease, and cost for most community clubs. A committee member can update news, add photos, and manage pages without calling a developer.

---

### Wix
**Cost:** ~$17–$25 AUD/month for business plans
**Best for:** Clubs that want maximum drag-and-drop flexibility.

**Pros:**
- Very flexible layout editor
- Large template library
- App market with extras (forms, booking, etc.)
- Free tier available (with Wix branding)

**Cons:**
- Quality of free templates varies widely — easy to build something that looks cheap
- SEO historically weaker than Squarespace (improving)
- Can get messy if multiple people edit

**Verdict:** Fine option. Requires more design taste to avoid looking generic.

---

### Webflow
**Cost:** ~$18–$36 USD/month
**Best for:** Clubs with a volunteer who has some design/tech confidence and wants a premium result.

**Pros:**
- Produces genuinely exceptional websites
- Full CSS control without writing code
- CMS for managing news/fixtures dynamically
- Best-in-class performance

**Cons:**
- Steeper learning curve — not for complete beginners
- Higher cost
- Needs a specific person to own it

**Verdict:** The highest-ceiling option. If you have the right person, Webflow produces a site that will genuinely impress sponsors.

---

### WordPress.com (Hosted)
**Cost:** ~$9–$45 USD/month
**Best for:** Clubs that want flexibility and already have some WordPress experience.

**Pros:**
- Massive plugin ecosystem
- Very flexible
- Large community / lots of support resources

**Cons:**
- Can become technically complex quickly
- Plugin maintenance can become a burden
- Security updates required
- Design quality varies widely by theme

**Verdict:** Avoid unless you have a dedicated tech volunteer. WordPress.org (self-hosted) is even more powerful but requires hosting management.

---

### Sportsground / SportsTG / PlayHQ Integrated Sites
**Cost:** Often bundled with association registration fees
**Best for:** Clubs that want fixtures, results, and ladders automatically populated.

**Pros:**
- Fixtures, results, ladders auto-populate from association data
- Low maintenance once set up
- Familiar to other clubs in the league

**Cons:**
- Often look generic / dated
- Limited design flexibility
- Sponsor presentation often poor
- May not support custom pages well

**Verdict:** Useful as a **secondary** tool (embed fixture/ladder widget), not as the primary website. Use a proper CMS for the main site, and embed a SportsTG/PlayHQ widget for live fixtures.

---

## Recommended Stack for a Community Football Club

**Primary website:** Squarespace (or Webflow if skilled volunteer available)
**Fixtures/results widget:** Embed from association platform (SportsTG, PlayHQ, etc.)
**Email:** Google Workspace for Nonprofits ($0 if eligible) or basic Gmail with custom domain (~$6/month via Google)
**Forms:** Built-in CMS forms (Squarespace/Webflow) or free Typeform
**Analytics:** Google Analytics 4 (free, critical for sponsor conversations)
**Social scheduling:** Buffer or Meta Business Suite (free tiers sufficient)

---

## Domain & Email Notes

**Always use a custom domain** — e.g., `cobrasfc.com.au` not `cobrasfc.wixsite.com/home`. Cost is ~$15–$25 AUD/year via Crazy Domains, VentraIP, or Cloudflare.

**Club email address matters.** `president@cobrasfc.com.au` reads as professional. `cobrasprez@gmail.com` does not. Use Google Workspace (check nonprofit eligibility at google.com/nonprofits) or Zoho Mail (free tier available).

A custom domain + professional email = immediate credibility lift at zero meaningful cost.
