# Club Photography Brief
## For Community Football Clubs — Commissioning a Photo Session

This brief is designed to share with a local photographer. One good session (2–4 hours) produces assets that will serve the club for 2–3 seasons across the website, social media, sponsorship prospectus, and print materials.

---

## Why Invest in Photography?

Real photography is the single highest-ROI design investment a community club can make. It transforms a generic website into one that communicates pride, identity, and credibility. Sponsors respond to it viscerally — it signals that the club takes itself seriously.

**Estimated cost:** $150–$400 AUD for a local photographer (half-day).
**Alternatively:** A volunteer with a good camera (Sony/Canon mirrorless, or even a recent iPhone in good light) can produce adequate results with this brief.

---

## Session Types

### Session 1: Match Day (Highest Priority)
**When:** Home game, good weather preferred.
**Duration:** 90 minutes (30 min pre-game + 60 min during game)

**Must-have shots:**
- [ ] Wide establishing shot of the ground (captures the whole scene — players warming up, spectators, signage)
- [ ] Hero action shot: a player in full kit, with club badge clearly visible, mid-action (running, kicking, heading)
- [ ] Team huddle / coach addressing players pre-game
- [ ] Goal celebration or emotional moment
- [ ] Supporter/spectator shot — families, kids watching, people cheering
- [ ] Close-up of kit badge/crest
- [ ] Handshake / sportsmanship moment

**What to avoid:**
- Blurry action shots (use burst mode, shutter speed 1/500s+)
- Backlit shots where faces are dark
- Cluttered backgrounds with distracting signage

---

### Session 2: Portrait / Committee (High Value for Sponsors)
**When:** Any weekday or pre-match.
**Duration:** 45 minutes

**Must-have shots:**
- [ ] President / Club Captain headshot (clean background, club kit or polo)
- [ ] Small group of 3–5 committee members (approachable, professional)
- [ ] Existing sponsor representative with a club representative (if available — gold for the prospectus)

**Style:** Confident and approachable. Slight smiles. Not stiff corporate — but not casual selfies either.

---

### Session 3: Juniors / Community (Sponsor Heartstrings)
**When:** Junior training session.
**Duration:** 45 minutes

**Must-have shots:**
- [ ] Kids in full kit, having fun — wide shot showing the number of participants
- [ ] Coach instructing a young player (mentorship signal)
- [ ] Parent watching from the sideline with pride
- [ ] Kids celebrating a goal or skill moment

**Note:** Ensure you have appropriate photography consents from parents/guardians. Most clubs include this in registration forms — confirm with your registrar.

---

## Technical Specifications

Ask the photographer to deliver:
- **Format:** JPG + RAW (if possible)
- **Resolution:** Minimum 3000px on the long edge (for print flexibility)
- **Colour profile:** sRGB (standard for web use)
- **Editing:** Light natural editing — boost contrast, correct white balance. Avoid heavy filters or over-saturation.
- **Delivery:** Dropbox/Google Drive link within 2 weeks

---

## Using the Photos

| Asset | Primary photos to use |
|---|---|
| Website hero (homepage) | Match day action, wide ground shot |
| About page | Committee portraits, ground establishing shot |
| Junior section | Juniors training session shots |
| Sponsors page | Group shot with sponsor rep, badge close-up |
| Social media | Action shots, goal celebrations, team huddle |
| Sponsorship prospectus | Hero action shot + community/junior shot |

---

## A Note on Consistency

Once you have a quality library of photos, use them consistently. Resist the temptation to mix in blurry phone photos alongside professional shots on the same page — the contrast undermines both.

Create a shared Google Drive folder called `[Club Name] — Official Photos [Year]` and make it the single source of truth for anyone updating the website, social media, or printed materials.
