# SEO Checklist for Community Football Club Websites

Community club websites don't need complex SEO strategies. They need to be findable by the people who matter: local families searching for a club to join, local businesses researching sponsorship opportunities, and journalists or community organisations looking for contacts.

This checklist covers the 20% of SEO work that delivers 80% of the results.

---

## Priority 1: Foundational (Do These First)

### Technical Basics
- [ ] **HTTPS active** — Check that the URL shows `https://` not `http://`. If not, fix immediately via your hosting provider (usually free).
- [ ] **Mobile-responsive** — Visit the site on a phone. If it looks broken, it's a priority fix.
- [ ] **Page load under 3 seconds** — Test at pagespeed.web.dev. Score 70+ on mobile is acceptable; 85+ is good.
- [ ] **No broken links** — Check with a free tool like deadlinkchecker.com
- [ ] **Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console** — Free tool, worth 30 minutes of setup.
- [ ] **Google Search Console connected** — Tells you how many people find you via Google and what they searched.

### On-Page Basics
- [ ] **Page title includes club name and location** — e.g., `Cobras FC | Football Club in Brunswick, VIC`
- [ ] **Meta description written** — 150–160 characters summarising the homepage. E.g., "Cobras FC is a community football club in Brunswick, Victoria, fielding junior and senior teams in the Northern Football League."
- [ ] **H1 heading on homepage** — One clear top-level heading that includes the club name.
- [ ] **Alt text on key images** — Describe important images for accessibility and search. e.g., `alt="Cobras FC players celebrating a goal at Allard Park"`

---

## Priority 2: Local SEO (Highest Impact for Clubs)

Local SEO is about being found by people in your suburb and surrounding areas.

### Google Business Profile (Free — Do This)
- [ ] **Create/claim a Google Business Profile** at business.google.com
- [ ] Fill in: Club name, address (ground address), phone, website URL, category ("Sports Club" or "Soccer Club")
- [ ] Add photos — use your best club photography
- [ ] Add opening hours for the clubroom if applicable
- [ ] This makes the club appear in Google Maps and the local search panel

### Local Keywords (Naturally Include These in Page Content)
Include these phrases naturally in page copy — don't force them:
- "[Suburb] football club"
- "[Suburb] soccer club"
- "Football club [suburb/region]"
- "[Association name] football"
- "Junior football [suburb]"
- "Register for football [suburb/year]"

### Local Citations
- [ ] Club listed on Football Victoria / FFA / state association website
- [ ] Club listed on local council community directory (most councils have one)
- [ ] Club listed on PlayHQ / SportsTG directory
- [ ] Club Facebook page with correct address and website URL

---

## Priority 3: Content SEO (Ongoing)

Search engines favour sites that are regularly updated with useful content.

### News/Blog Posts
Each news post is a potential search landing page. Good post topics:
- Season launch announcements
- Player registrations open / "How to register for [Club Name] [Year]"
- Match reports (include player names and suburb references)
- Committee and volunteer announcements
- Community partnership news

**Minimum:** 1 post per month during the season.
**Ideal:** 2–4 posts per month.

### Pages Worth Having (Each Targets Different Searches)
- [ ] **About page** — Club history, values, who you are
- [ ] **Teams page** — One page per team if possible (e.g., "Cobras FC Senior Men's" page)
- [ ] **Fixtures & Results** — Keep updated; search engines index this
- [ ] **Join Us page** — Targets "how to join [club name]" and "junior football [suburb]" searches
- [ ] **Sponsors page** — Targets "[club name] sponsorship" searches from businesses researching the club
- [ ] **Contact page** — Include physical ground address for local SEO

---

## Priority 4: Measuring Progress

Set these up once and check monthly:

| Tool | What it tells you | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search Console | How many people find you via Google, what they searched | Free |
| Google Analytics 4 | How many people visit your site, which pages | Free |
| Google Business Profile Insights | How many people found you on Maps | Free |

**The Sponsor Data Point:** Once Analytics is running, you can tell a prospective sponsor: "Our website receives approximately [X] visitors per month from [suburbs]." This is a meaningful metric that converts hesitant sponsors.

---

## What NOT to Worry About

Community club websites don't need:
- Keyword density analysis
- Backlink building campaigns
- Schema markup (nice to have, not essential)
- International SEO
- Paid search advertising

Focus on being genuinely findable by local people. That's all that matters.
