🎨 Metford Cobras — Rematch-Inspired Image Style Guide Snippet (Updated)

Use a vibrant, hyper-stylised sports illustration style inspired by Rematch.
Key characteristics:

⚡ Overall Rendering Style

Cel-shaded realism with clean edge highlights

Bold graphic shadows (not soft, not photorealistic)

Slight painterly texture in grass, sky, uniforms, and backgrounds

Dynamic, diagonal compositions that emphasise action and speed

Use motion streaks, arcs, and energy lines to convey movement

🎨 Colour Palette (Cobra-Aligned)

Base your colour palette on the official Metford Cobras colours:

Blue — #253D97 (primary, deep and bold)

Yellow — #ECDD14 (accents and highlights, bright and energetic)

How to apply them in this art style:

Use Blue (#253D97) for kits, shadows, environmental accents, signage

Use Yellow (#ECDD14) for highlight streaks, edge glows, light accents, uniform details

Allow supplementary colours (greens, warm golds, magentas) but always keep Cobras blue + yellow dominant

Lighting stays bright, sun-kissed, slightly warm

🔠 Typography (for contextual consistency only)

(Even though these fonts won’t appear in generated images unless text is requested, include for style coherence across the brand.)

Primary font: Poppins Medium

Secondary font: Baskervville Italic

Use this typographic pairing as reference for the graphic tone: modern, clean, with a touch of tradition and community warmth.

No need to include font shapes unless text is part of the image request—this simply guides the “feel” of the image.

⚽ Subject & Composition Guidelines

Players depicted as heroic, energetic, dynamic community athletes

Use authentic local football kit shapes, but in the stylised Cobras colours

Emphasise expressive movement: sprints, tackles, goal celebrations, teamwork

Slight exaggeration of poses to amplify excitement (e.g., longer limbs, bigger movement arcs)

🌤️ Backgrounds & Environment

Use stylised versions of real community football environments:

Local fields

Australian gum trees

Warm afternoon sun

Low-rise club buildings

Keep backgrounds simplified with graphic shapes and atmospheric depth

Avoid sci-fi or futuristic architecture; keep it grounded, familiar, and local but visually elevated

💛 Tone & Emotional Feel

Energetic

Family-friendly

Aspirational but grounded

Community heroes framed in blockbuster sports style
